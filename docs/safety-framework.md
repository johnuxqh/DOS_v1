# Safety Framework

Deck of Sweat uses conservative defaults and clear scaling language. It is not medical care or individualized coaching.

## RPE

Rate of perceived exertion (RPE) is a 1-10 effort scale:

- **RPE 4-5:** Easy to moderate; several reps remain in reserve.
- **RPE 6-7:** Sustainable training effort; breathing increases but technique stays crisp.
- **RPE 8:** Hard but controlled; stop before form breakdown.
- **RPE 9-10:** Near-maximal or maximal effort; not the default target for micro-workouts.

## Scaling

Every active exercise should include a regression and a progression. Scaling can adjust range of motion, load, speed, stability demand, impact, or total volume.

## Beginner safety

Beginner-safe exercises must have difficulty level 3 or below. Beginner sessions should prioritize low-impact movements, stable positions, simple instructions, and conservative pacing.

## High-impact caution

High-impact and plyometric movements can be useful for prepared athletes but should be limited. They are not beginner-safe by default and should be avoided when joint pain, poor landing control, or fatigue is present.

## Pain rule

Discomfort from effort is different from pain. Stop any movement that causes sharp, worsening, radiating, or joint-specific pain. Substitute a regression or choose a different card.

## Medical disclaimer wording

Deck of Sweat content is for general fitness education only and is not medical advice. Users should consult a qualified healthcare professional before starting exercise if they have medical conditions, injuries, symptoms, or concerns.
