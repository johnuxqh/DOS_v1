# Exercise Taxonomy

Deck of Sweat classifies exercises so workouts can be balanced, scalable, and safe.

## Movement patterns

- **Squat:** Knee-dominant lower-body work such as air squats, goblet squats, lunges, and step-ups.
- **Hinge:** Hip-dominant work such as glute bridges, deadlifts, and Romanian deadlifts.
- **Push:** Upper-body pushing such as push-ups and presses.
- **Pull:** Upper-body pulling such as rows and band pull-aparts.
- **Carry:** Loaded walking or holding patterns that challenge posture, grip, trunk control, and conditioning.
- **Rotate:** Controlled trunk or hip rotation.
- **Anti-rotate:** Resisting rotation, such as dead bugs, side planks, and Pallof presses.
- **Locomotion:** Traveling bodyweight work such as crawls or mountain climbers.
- **Jump:** Explosive jumping and landing patterns.
- **Mobility:** Controlled range-of-motion drills.

## Why movement patterns matter

Movement patterns help the generator avoid accidental overuse, create balanced sessions, and preserve variety. A four-card workout is more useful when it does not repeat the same joint stress four times. Pattern tagging also helps scale workouts for beginners, equipment constraints, and protocol types.
