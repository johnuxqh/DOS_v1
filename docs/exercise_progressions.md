# Exercise Progressions and Regressions

Exercise progressions define deterministic relationships between exercises that make a movement harder, easier, or meaningfully different.

- A **progression** increases challenge through load, volume, tempo, complexity, range of motion, stability, impact, equipment, or skill.
- A **regression** reduces or simplifies a challenge while retaining a useful movement goal.
- A **lateral variation** changes the exercise or equipment without intending to change overall difficulty.

## Relationship to workout composition

Workout composition rules decide which roles a balanced workout should contain, such as lower body, push, pull, and core. Exercise progression records do not change those roles. They connect one exercise to another option that can fill a similar role at a different challenge level or with different equipment.

## Future coaching and personalisation

The exported relationships can later support coaching suggestions, progression plans, exercise substitutions, and personalised workout adjustments. Coaching and safety notes travel with each relationship so future tools can explain the suggestion rather than only naming another exercise.

## Not user tracking

This data describes exercise relationships only. It does not store workout history, user performance, subscriptions, accounts, or decisions about when a specific user should progress. A future coaching system can use these relationships alongside appropriate user context and safety checks.
